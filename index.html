Mailroom

Mailroom is a personal, single-user tool that reads, labels, and drafts mail only in its owner's own Gmail account.

Privacy Policy

Mailroom accesses only the Gmail account of its single owner. Mail content is processed by an AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to classify messages and draft replies for the owner; it is not shared, sold, or used for anything else. There is no tracking and no analytics on this page or within the tool.
